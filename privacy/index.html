Nago Studio Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Nago Studio Discord bot (“the Bot”) may process and why.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, the Bot may process:

  • Discord user IDs;
  • Discord server, channel and role IDs;
  • usernames or display names when required for Discord functionality;
  • quote or project request information voluntarily submitted by you;
  • support or ticket information voluntarily submitted by you;
  • timestamps and request/status information;
  • role selections and user preferences;
  • limited administrative and audit information necessary to operate the Bot.

The Bot is not designed to collect passwords, authentication credentials, payment card information, or other highly sensitive information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only when reasonably necessary to:

  • provide Bot functionality;
  • manage quote and project enquiries;
  • operate support tickets;
  • manage Discord roles and server infrastructure;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse;
  • diagnose technical problems;
  • maintain appropriate administrative records.

3. Message Content

The Bot does not intentionally store general Discord conversation content unless a specific feature requires information that a user deliberately submits, such as a support ticket or quote request.

4. Storage

Bot data may be stored in Nago Studio-controlled databases or infrastructure.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, operational requirements, dispute resolution, security, or applicable legal obligations.

Closed tickets, enquiries, or related records may periodically be deleted or anonymised when they are no longer required.

5. Sharing of Information

Nago Studio does not sell personal information.

Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary:

  • with authorised Nago Studio staff who need access to provide the requested service;
  • with infrastructure or service providers required to operate the Bot;
  • when required by applicable law or a valid legal request;
  • to protect users, Nago Studio, or the security of the service.

6. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord. Discord separately processes information according to its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, including the GDPR where applicable, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

You may contact Nago Studio regarding information associated with you that is stored by the Bot.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot gains or changes functionality. The latest version will always be available at this page.
